Tiling Layout Prototype

The question. Can scopecreep's Window behave like a tiling window manager: one panel fills the app, each new one splits the focused panel along its longer side, you can add a panel from any edge or drag one where you want it, the same display can appear four times, one press of Auto layout can re-arrange the whole Window for the footage's shape, and you can keep several pages, each with its own layout? This page is a throwaway stand-in for the layout rules only. The pictures, waveform and vectorscope are real maths on a made-up frame, but nothing here measures a real Feed.

Click a panel to focus it (light outline). Add panel splits the focused panel along its longer side. Hover a panel's edge for a +. Drag a title onto another panel: edges split, the middle makes a tab. Drag the gaps to resize. Use ⋯ for replace, duplicate and close. Auto layout is a one-shot button: it re-arranges every panel on this page right now (Undo brings the old layout back). Auto resize only resizes the splits. Both act on the current page only, which is why they sit with the page tabs. Alt+1 to 9 switch pages.
