ScopeCreep for macOS (Apple Silicon)
====================================

The installer puts the ScopeCreep app in /Applications and the Sender (the
plugin that sends DaVinci Resolve's picture to the app) in
/Library/OFX/Plugins, where Resolve looks for plugins.

What you need
  A Mac with an Apple Silicon chip (M1 or later) and DaVinci Resolve. Intel
  Macs are not supported: the installer stops with a message on one.
  Resolve's GPU processing can stay on Metal (the default).

Opening the installer
  The installer is not signed or notarised yet, so after you download it in a
  browser macOS will not open it with a double-click. Either:
    - right-click (or Control-click) scopecreep-X.Y.Z-macos-arm64.pkg,
      choose Open, then Open again in the window that appears; or
    - double-click it, dismiss the warning, then open System Settings >
      Privacy & Security, scroll to the message about the installer and
      click "Open Anyway".
  macOS asks for your password, as the Sender goes in a folder shared by
  every user.

Install
  1. Close DaVinci Resolve. If it stays open it will not load the new Sender.
  2. Open the installer as above and follow it.
  3. Start DaVinci Resolve. On the Color page, add ScopeCreep from
     Effects > OpenFX to a node (a serial node at the end of your grade),
     then open ScopeCreep from Launchpad, Spotlight or the Applications
     folder.
  Installing a newer version over an older one replaces it.

Uninstall
  Close DaVinci Resolve, then delete these two items in Finder (Go > Go to
  Folder...):
    /Applications/ScopeCreep.app
    /Library/OFX/Plugins/scopecreep_sender.ofx.bundle
  and start Resolve again. Your settings stay in
  ~/Library/Application Support/ScopeCreep; delete that folder to remove them
  too. This note is also kept in /Library/Application Support/ScopeCreep.

Check your download
  In Terminal:  shasum -a 256 scopecreep-X.Y.Z-macos-arm64.pkg
  and compare it with the line in SHA256SUMS (the file is on the Releases
  page).
