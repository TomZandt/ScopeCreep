# #206: Add panel, Close, the focus outline and the empty page

What ticket #206 showed on 2026-11-20, at the pure layout seam (`app/tests/add_close.rs`, `app/tests/layout_tree.rs`) and the pure UI seam (`app/tests/panel_add_ui.rs`, `app/tests/menu_bar_seam.rs`, `app/tests/commands_seam.rs`), headless. Nothing was checked at the app or in Resolve: how the outline, the Add list and the empty-page button look and feel on a real monitor is still to be seen by a person.

- Add panel splits the focused cell along its longer side at exactly 50/50 (a square cell counts as wide, so the new Panel goes on the right). The expected cells are worked out independently in the tests. Only the focused cell changes; every other Panel stays where it was. The new Panel has the focus. On an empty page the new Panel fills the whole area.
- No room is judged on the focused cell alone: half of it (net of the gap) under the minimum width (side by side) or height (stacked) leaves the tree, the focus and the cells untouched and gives a notice, "No room for another Panel here: a Panel needs at least 120 x 105." (logical pixels, from the minimum Panel size). Exactly twice the minimum plus the gap is enough.
- Close removes the Panel and its sibling (which may be a whole group of Panels) takes the space with its own shares kept; the column or row still ends where it did. The focus moves to the closed Panel's neighbour (the first Panel of its sibling) when it had it. Closing the last Panel leaves an empty page.
- The saved text lists only the Panels on the page, so a closed Panel stays closed after a restart; an empty page is the line `empty`. The focus is session only and does not make one layout differ from another (`LayoutTree` equality compares the arrangement). A saved layout that lacks a display the Window has now keeps that display closed (before, it gave the default layout); duplicates, unknown Panels and rubbish behave as before, and an old "layout 1" grid still has to hold every display to convert.
- Every Panel's title bar now has the settings button, because every popover ends with a Panel section holding Close (also reachable by right-click). Add panel and Close are Commands without hotkeys; the Layout menu has "Add panel…" (the list of displays, then Back) and "Close panel", so nothing needs a drag.
- The Window has the seven displays created at start-up, so a display already on the page is greyed in the Add list; a second instance of one needs a display made at run time and comes with Duplicate (#208). Until #211 no-room is refused instead of joining a tab group.
- The Window's minimum size counts the Add list (seven names) with the other menus.
