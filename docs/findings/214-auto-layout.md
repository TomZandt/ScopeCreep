# #214: Auto layout

What ticket #214 showed on 2026-11-20, at the pure layout seam (`app/tests/auto_layout.rs`) and the pure UI seam (`app/tests/page_bar.rs`, `app/tests/menu_bar_seam.rs`, `app/tests/commands_seam.rs`), headless. Nothing was checked at the app: the button on a real window and how the result looks on a real page are still to be tried by a person.

- The search is `LayoutTree::auto_layout(items, aspect, area, gap, scale)` (the search in `app/src/layout_tree/arrange.rs`, the weights and the score in `app/src/auto_layout.rs`). `items` come from `DisplaySet::items()`, in Panel order. It returns `Err(NoFit)` and changes nothing when no cut keeps every Panel at the least size; `NoFit::notice()` names that size (120 x 105 logical pixels).
- The items are the page's tab groups in reading order (a tab group is one item and scores as its shown Panel). Up to 6 items every cutting and direction is tried; at 7 to 9 only the first cuts near the middle (half, and half rounded up) are, with every cutting below them; above 9 there is no search: the ordered list is halved (the first half the larger), each cut across the longer side of the room it has, shares from the weights. The fallback is also dropped, with the notice, when it breaks a minimum.
- Each candidate gets its shares from the weights (clean picture 3, luma waveform 1.6, Vectorscope 1.2, anything else 1), is fitted as Auto resize fits, then every share is held where neither side goes under its minimum (the prototype only checked the sum of minimums; here a ratio that would break one is moved). A candidate whose minimums do not fit the area at all is dropped before it is fitted.
- Score is the sum of weight times ln(1 + useful area). Useful area is the fitted frame less the legend strip for a Picture View, the aspect-true plot less the 44 px gutter (and the legend strip) for the luma waveform, the inscribed circle of the content area for the Vectorscope, and the whole content area for any other display (the colour waveform, the CIE chart). The prototype treated those last as picture-shaped; here they are not.
- Ties go to the cutting generated first, so it is deterministic, and a second press changes nothing (no undo step). The clean picture has the most room for the default 7 Panels at 16:9, 2.39:1 and 9:16 footage in a 1256 x 716 Window, in a portrait Window and at 2x. The result differs between 16:9, 2.39:1, 9:16 and a portrait Window.
- Time, measured at the seam on a dev build (optimised, as the workspace profile sets): 7 Panels about 3.5 ms (worst of 5), 9 Panels about 60 ms at 2512 x 1432, the 12 Panel fallback under the 100 ms the test allows. The spec's budget was about 100 ms at 7 Panels; the search is run on the UI thread on a button press, never on the Feed thread.
- The Page bar has an Auto layout chip left of Auto resize (the tabs give up the room); "Auto layout" is in the Layout menu above Auto resize and is the Command "Page: auto layout", with no default hotkey. Nothing but the button, the menu entry and the Command calls it, once, through `Pages::change`: one undo step on the current page, the focused Panel unchanged by it and by its undo; a press that finds no fit is no undo step and shows the notice.
