# #211: Tab groups: dock in the middle, tabs, and hidden tabs do not measure

What ticket #211 showed on 2026-11-20, at the pure layout seam (`app/tests/tab_groups.rs`, plus the updated `add_close.rs`, `edge_add.rs`, `dock_layout.rs`, `undo_layout.rs`), the pure UI seam (`app/tests/tab_strip_ui.rs`) and the Feed seam (`app/tests/display_seam.rs`, needs the GPU). Nothing was checked at the app or in Resolve: how the tabs, the "Tab here" overlay and the notice look and feel on a real monitor, and that a click on a tab or the overflow chip does what the pure tests say, is still to be seen by a person.

- A drop in the middle of a tab group (`join_tab`) makes the dragged Panel the last tab and the shown one; its old tab group gives up its space to its neighbour, or just loses the tab. A drop on its own group only shows it. Clicking a tab (`show`) shows that Panel and focuses it; the shown tab keeps its place in the saved text (`leaf <shown> a|b|c`), so a restart gives the same tab group.
- A tab dragged out is the existing dock: the others stay, and the tab group they leave is as it was. The shown tab can be dragged onto its own group's edge too; the next tab then takes its place. Closing the shown tab shows the tab that takes its place (the next, else the one before); closing a hidden tab leaves the shown one shown.
- No room is no longer a refusal. An Add panel, an edge add or a side dock that would break a minimum returns `Placed::Tabbed`: the Panel is a new tab of the focused (or target) tab group, shown and focused, with a notice ("No room for another Panel here, so it opened as a tab: a Panel needs at least 120 x 105.", and "No room to dock here, so it joined as a tab: ..." for a dock). Each is one undo step and the Panel is never lost. Duplicate (#208) still refuses with its own notice, because a copy as a tab beside its original is of no use.
- Hidden Panels do not measure. `DisplaySet::set_shown` (given `LayoutTree::shown_panels`) marks every Panel that is closed or in a hidden tab, and `on_frame` and `on_timeline_frame` skip them: a Recorder display in a hidden tab was handed no frame while its neighbour was, and the built-in waveform still counted every pixel. This also stops closed first instances measuring, which they did until now. A change bumps the set's generation, so the Feed thread configures and measures the frame on screen again and a Panel shown again has a picture even with Resolve stopped. Settings and state are untouched.
- The tab strip is drawn in the title bar's room between the left edge and the first chip (or the settings button): the shown tab has the control fill and an accent underline, the others are quiet and light on hover. Tabs take their natural width up to 160 logical px; squeezed equally down to 34 px before any overflow; beyond that the shown tab and as many others as fit stay, and a "+N" chip counts the rest (a click on it shows the next hidden tab, wrapping). At the minimum Panel width (120 px) a title bar with the settings button and one chip has room for only one tab and the chip. A tab group's title bar shows no name and no tags (the tabs replace them).
- The drop overlay's middle zone is labelled "Tab here" (the whole target Panel is outlined); an edge shows the half it would take, as before.
- ADR 0003's performance check was not re-run at the app; hidden Panels only reduce the work, and the feed-loop and display seams that include every Panel shown still pass.
